which are part of the data's evidentiary value.

## Disclaimer of warranty

The records describe historical claims. They were compiled by cross-checking
printed local gazetteers, place-name references and published lists of martyrs,
and every entry lists its sources — but compilation was done by volunteers
without access to every archive, and **no warranty of historical accuracy is
offered or implied**.

Known open questions and methodological caveats are listed in
[`KNOWN_ISSUES.md`](./KNOWN_ISSUES.md). If you verify a record against a source
we could not reach, please open an issue or a pull request: corrections are the
most valuable contribution this dataset can receive.

## A note on dual licensing

Splitting code (MIT) from data (CC BY-SA 4.0) is intentional. Permissive code
licences are a poor fit for editorially compiled historical data: the
ShareAlike clause is what keeps corrections flowing back into the public copy
rather than being absorbed into closed derivatives, and the Attribution clause
keeps the compilers and the cited gazetteers visible wherever the data travels.
